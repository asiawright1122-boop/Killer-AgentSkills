# Requirements: Killer-Skills Agent Directory

**Defined:** 2026-05-29
**Status:** Active milestone: `v2.0 Helpful Content Injection and Authority Unlock`
**Core Value:** Maximize discoverability and operational reliability of AI developer tooling through unattended, auditable content enrichment, publish, and verification loops.

## v2.0 Requirements

### Helpful Content Injection

- [ ] **SEO-16**: Operator can draft and inject original helpful-content editorial copy (at least 250 words) on the homepage.
- [ ] **CTR-03**: Operator can craft specific expert setup examples and recommendations on at least 3 featured collection detail hubs.

### Physical GSC Coverage Refresh

- [ ] **REC-30**: Operator can ingest a physical GSC Coverage report dated post-May 2026.
- [ ] **REC-31**: Operator can clear the local Max Days override, restoring the sub-3-day freshness SLA and verifying error drop.

### Selective Authority Expansion

- [ ] **GEO-05**: Operator can selectively promote up to 5 top-performing holding authority surfaces.
- [ ] **REC-32**: Operator can onboard at least 3 high-value, organic, non-paid backlinks and verify traffic outcomes in the 2026-06 proof window.

## v1.9 Requirements (Satisfied)

### Search Compliance

- [x] **SEO-15**: Operator can review a project-specific compliance matrix that maps Google Search Essentials, Bing Webmaster guidance, IndexNow, and Yandex IndexNow expectations to crawl, index, canonical, redirect, content-quality, structured-data, CTR, and AI-search evidence.

### Measurement Freshness

- [x] **REC-26**: Operator can ingest a fresh Coverage Drilldown export inside the hard freshness SLA and regenerate recovery reports from it.

### Manual Recovery Execution

- [x] **REC-27**: Operator can execute the P0 other-cluster and source-file recovery batches with explicit keep-410, redirect-validation, and recrawl-watch outcomes.
- [x] **REC-28**: Operator can verify trailing-slash, query-parameter, repeated-segment, and deep-skill-path canonicalization against fresh Coverage evidence.

### Proof Window

- [x] **REC-29**: Operator can collect a post-intervention proof window that distinguishes crawl health, Coverage cluster movement, GSC demand movement, authority promotion readiness, and automation readiness.

### CTR and AI Search Visibility

- [x] **CTR-02**: Operator can identify priority authority surfaces with click-through opportunity, update only user-facing title/snippet/heading copy where evidence supports it, and verify the rendered metadata remains accurate and non-manipulative.
- [x] **GEO-04**: Operator can capture Bing AI Performance / IndexNow / AI-search visibility evidence when available, or record a clear unavailable-data state without inventing citation or AI traffic proof.

## Still Out Of Scope

| Feature | Reason |
|---------|--------|
| Paid Workers AI expansion | Budget and policy remain unchanged; recovery remains evidence-first, not provider-expansion work |
| Bulk skill-detail re-expansion | Index volume is still subordinate to evidence-backed authority recovery |
| Net-new authority surfaces | Existing surfaces must earn promotion before adding breadth |
| Automation rollout | No experiment has repeatable proof or automation-candidate status yet |
| Search-engine-first content rewrites | Google helpful-content guidance and the v1.7 copy guardrail both require user-facing usefulness over process or ranking language |
