## Practical Compliance Themes

### Crawl and Index Eligibility

- Search engines need crawlable links, accessible page resources, and clear sitemap/canonical signals.
- Sitemaps should list preferred canonical URLs, not alternate trap URLs.
- Robots, noindex, redirects, canonical tags, and status codes must agree; conflicting signals delay or weaken recovery.

### Content Quality and Trust

- Recovery work must improve usefulness, clarity, originality, and trust, not merely add or churn pages for freshness.
- Public pages must remain people-first and user-facing; internal planning language, search-process language, and unverifiable recovery claims are trust risks.
- Helpful content guidance makes freshness manipulation a risk: dates and claims should change only when content materially changes.

### Canonicalization and Redirects

- Permanent server-side redirects are the safest signal when a URL has permanently moved.
- Canonical tags and sitemap URLs should reinforce the same preferred URL.
- Temporary redirects, JavaScript redirects, and conflicting canonical hints are risky for recovery diagnostics.

### CTR and Search Appearance

- Titles should be unique, concise, accurate, and aligned with visible headings.
- Snippet influence depends on page copy and sometimes meta descriptions; public surface copy should answer the query cleanly instead of exposing operational process.
- Structured data should be valid only where it reflects visible page content.

### AI Search and GEO

- Bing AI Performance now exposes citation-oriented evidence across Copilot, Bing AI summaries, and supported partner integrations.
- Bing frames AI visibility as cited pages, grounding queries, and citation trends rather than ranking or traffic proof.
- IndexNow can notify participating engines when URLs are added, changed, or deleted; it does not guarantee indexing.

## v1.9 Implications

- Create a project-specific compliance matrix before changing more pages, so fixes align with search-engine rules instead of ad hoc SEO instincts.
- Treat Coverage Drilldown freshness as a hard gate for cluster-level claims.
- Keep P0 URL work manual until redirect, canonical, sitemap, GSC, and Coverage signals agree.
- Add a CTR review for priority authority surfaces, but only with user-facing copy and measurable GSC before/after evidence.
- Add Bing AI Performance / IndexNow evidence if exports or verified access are available; otherwise mark as unavailable rather than inventing AI visibility proof.
