## Archive Note

This archive preserves the milestone-scoped requirements that were active for `v1.5`, the final traceability used during closeout, and the carry-forward themes for the next milestone. Fresh active requirements should be defined only when the next milestone is started.

## Shipped Requirements

### Traffic Evidence Freshness

- [x] **SEO-14**: A fresh Search Console summary plus page/query opportunity report is available at least weekly in repository-local artifacts so business recovery can be judged from current click and impression data.
- [x] **SEO-15**: Coverage Drilldown raw exports and the normalized drilldown report stay inside a 7-day freshness window, with stale or missing inputs surfaced explicitly instead of silently degrading recovery attribution.

### Recovery Diagnosis

- [x] **SEO-16**: Recovery reporting attributes traffic and indexing gaps by page, query, locale, and issue cluster so operators can separate true demand loss from measurement gaps or already-closed crawl debt.
- [x] **GOV-10**: Operators have a ranked recovery-control board that separates blocked, recoverable, and already-recovered surfaces with explicit evidence and next actions.

### Recovery Execution

- [x] **GEO-01**: The highest-impact recovery surfaces can be converted into an execution queue with concrete metadata, content-refresh, and internal-link actions plus tracked outcome notes.

### Structural Search Governance

- [x] **SEO-17**: Skill locale variants are indexable only when title, description, and crawler-visible primary body are genuinely localized; ineligible locale variants are consolidated or excluded instead of self-canonicalizing.
- [x] **SEO-18**: Skill detail pages must pass a first-party value contract before indexing, proving original evaluation beyond imported GitHub or repository content.
- [x] **SEO-19**: The public skill corpus can be reduced to a governed keep/consolidate/noindex/remove set, and the resulting rollout is validated against coverage and traffic evidence.
- [x] **GOV-11**: Skill sitemap, canonical, hreflang, and noindex outputs agree on the reduced, auditable indexable corpus at both locale and page level.

### Authority Rebuild

- [x] **SEO-20**: Organic recovery is re-centered on a smaller set of high-confidence authority surfaces such as collections, official skills, comparisons, and workflow/tutorial pages instead of bulk skill detail expansion.

## Outcome Notes

- `v1.5` closed with `10/10` requirements satisfied, `8/8` roadmap phases complete, and a passed milestone audit.
- The milestone shipped truthful recovery capabilities, but not a declaration of traffic recovery; the latest scorecard still reports `businessRecoveryStatus=warning`.
- The governed public skill publish set is now `1099` kept routes out of `2950` pre-governance routes.
- Organic recovery emphasis now flows through `17` explicit authority surfaces, with `16` primary surfaces and one supporting directory surface.
- Workers AI remains `free-only`; paid expansion stayed intentionally out of scope.

## Carry-Forward Candidates

- **UX-EXP-01**: Reopen public discovery expansion only after traffic proof, governance rollout, and authority-surface recovery stay inside guardrails.
- **GEO-02**: Automate authority-surface recovery experiments only after the manual governance and editorial loop proves which interventions consistently move traffic.
- **AIOPS-12**: Promote workload-aware provider policy into operator-managed profiles with environment-specific defaults.
