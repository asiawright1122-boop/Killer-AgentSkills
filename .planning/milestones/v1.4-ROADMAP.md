# Milestone v1.4: Traffic Recovery Closure

**Status:** SHIPPED 2026-04-09
**Audit:** [v1.4-MILESTONE-AUDIT.md](./v1.4-MILESTONE-AUDIT.md)
**Phases:** 43-45

## Overview

This milestone turned the traffic crash response from ad-hoc technical repair into a closure program with durable evidence. The shipped result restored the live sitemap and sampled crawl to a clean technical state, made business-recovery gaps explicit in one recovery board, and formalized recovery-time AI fallback posture without weakening NVIDIA-primary routing or Workers AI `free-only` guardrails.

## Milestone Summary

### Key Accomplishments

1. Restored the production sitemap and live crawl sample to a clean technical recovery baseline.
2. Shipped a recovery scorecard that now makes missing business evidence explicit instead of hiding it behind technical stability.
3. Formalized provider backup posture across config, runtime, and operator reports while keeping Workers AI inside the free-only envelope.
4. Rebuilt the planning traceability and milestone support chain so `v1.4` could be audited and closed cleanly.
5. Closed the milestone with `5/5` requirements satisfied and a passed milestone audit.

### Key Decisions

- Keep technical recovery and business recovery as separate truths in the operator scorecard.
- Keep NVIDIA primary and Workers AI `free-only` even during recovery-time fallback hardening.
- Treat Cloudflare Workers AI as `burst-only` by default so free-only usage remains an explicit last-resort path.
- Prefer milestone-scoped recovery evidence over one-off operational checks so closure decisions stay reproducible.

### Issues Resolved

- Sitemap-linked and canonicalizable URL debt no longer dominates sampled crawl failures on the live domain.
- Recovery reporting no longer silently degrades when traffic or coverage evidence is missing.
- Backup-provider posture is no longer implicit or ambiguous during provider degradation.
- `v1.4` planning evidence is now machine-readable enough for traceability, closeout, and audit workflows.

### Deferred / Watchlist

- Traffic recovery still lacks fresh Search Console evidence, so business recovery remains unproven.
- Coverage attribution still depends on raw exports newer than the currently available `2026-04-03` drilldown snapshot.
- SiliconFlow remains in billing/access trouble and should either be restored or intentionally disabled in a future milestone.
- Growth work on public discovery surfaces should wait until traffic proof and recovery execution stabilize.
