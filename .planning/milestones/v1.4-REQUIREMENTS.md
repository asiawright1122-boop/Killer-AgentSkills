## Shipped Requirements

### Recovery Health

- [x] **SEO-11**: Production sitemap and sampled crawl on `killer-skills.com` stay at `5xx=0`, `Cloudflare 1102=0`, and sampled `4xx <= 0.2%` across the monitored recovery window.
- [x] **SEO-12**: Invalid, deep, repeated-segment, or trailing-slash skill-path variants are either excluded, redirected, or canonicalized so they do not recur as sampled crawl failures.

### Recovery Evidence

- [x] **SEO-13**: Coverage drilldown inputs and crawl-health outputs can be consolidated into a single recovery report that identifies dominant error clusters and their mitigation status.
- [x] **GOV-09**: Operators have a milestone-scoped recovery scorecard that combines crawl, coverage, index, and traffic signals with explicit weekly gates.

### AI Runtime Safety

- [x] **AIOPS-11**: Provider posture remains explicit during recovery: NVIDIA stays primary, unhealthy backups can be intentionally disabled or downgraded, and Workers AI remains provably constrained to the `free-only` envelope.

## Outcome Notes

- Technical recovery is now stable enough to be measured independently from business recovery.
- The operator lane now shows missing traffic and coverage evidence as explicit blockers or warnings instead of silently implying success.
- AI recovery posture is now explicit end-to-end, with Cloudflare held in `burst-only` and Workers AI still capped at `free-only 60/60`.
- `v1.4` closed with `5/5` requirements satisfied, `3/3` roadmap phases complete, and no blocking audit gaps.

## Carry-Forward Candidates

- **SEO-14**: Fresh Search Console summary and page/query opportunity data should be available weekly without manual export hunting.
- **SEO-15**: Coverage Drilldown raw exports should stay inside a freshness SLA with explicit missing-input handling.
- **SEO-16**: Recovery reporting should attribute traffic and indexing gaps by page, query, locale, and issue cluster instead of only global status.
- **GOV-10**: Operators should have a ranked recovery-control board that separates blocked, recoverable, and already-recovered surfaces.
- **GEO-01**: Highest-impact recovery surfaces should turn into an execution queue with measurable content, metadata, and internal-link actions.
- **UX-EXP-01**: Expand public discovery surfaces only after recovery KPIs remain green and operator noise stays bounded.
